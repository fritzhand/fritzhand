## 👋 About Me

*Catalyzing individuals and organizations to create positive impact.*

I believe the world gets better when more people have the tools, support, and confidence to build things. So I build things, I help others build things, and sometimes I build the things that help others build things.

I'm **VP of Platform & Member Engagement at [Embarc Collective](https://embarccollective.com)**, the startup hub in Downtown Tampa. 📍 Tampa Bay, by way of Ahmedabad, Bagru, and Cincinnati.

## 🛠️ What I'm Building

### 🌌 [The Nexus](https://wearenexus.xyz) · wearenexus.xyz

A shared 3D universe where every startup community's map is a planet you can travel to. Each map runs on **[portfolio-map-3d](https://github.com/fritzhand/portfolio-map-3d)**, my open-source framework that turns an accelerator cohort, an incubator's members, a fund's portfolio, or a region's startups into a turning globe, a world you walk through, and a searchable directory. The network has seven maps so far, from Tampa to Ahmedabad. Build your own, and join with a one-file pull request.

### 🏝️ [Embarc Member Map](https://community.embarccollective.com) · community.embarccollective.com

All **127** Embarc Collective member companies, sorted into **15** industry districts, plus our **18** coaches. You can explore them as a rotating globe or a campus you walk through, or use the provider finder to ask whether a member can do something for you. This is Embarc's planet in the Nexus.

### 🚀 [Quickstart](https://fritzhand.github.io/quickstart/)

Publish your first website by telling an AI agent what you want. You type or dictate, and Claude Code or Codex does the work. Five steps: a GitHub account, an agent, just enough Git, a starter to make your own, and GitHub Pages. It's written for people who have never shipped a site.

### 📚 [startup-stack](https://github.com/fritzhand/startup-stack)

The documents every startup should have, compiled into a knowledge base an AI can actually read. Put your deck, research, and call notes in one folder. One prompt turns them into a ten-section stack, and 19 more prompts run on top of it: the pitch deck, the list of 100, unit economics, the weekly recap. It's plain markdown, needs no code, and works with any AI that can read your files. **[Read it as a site →](https://fritzhand.github.io/startup-stack/)**

<sub>All four share the same habits: open source, plain files, every fact keeps its source, and unknowns stay blank instead of guessed.</sub>

## 🛤️ How I Got Here

Over the years I've built startup incubators, an artisan enterprise, a peace-tech platform, and AI tools.

- 🧵 &nbsp;Co-founded **Bagru Textiles**, now **[Studio Bagru](https://studiobagru.com)**. For fifteen years it has connected Rajasthan's hand block printing artisans to global markets.
- 🕊️ &nbsp;Founded **[PeaceRise](https://peacerise.app)**, a peace-tech platform that grew to 200+ partners across 26 countries.
- 🌱 &nbsp;Director of Startup Services at **Alloy Growth Lab** in Greater Cincinnati.
- 🏛️ &nbsp;CEO of **VentureStudio**, the innovation and incubation platform at **Ahmedabad University**.
